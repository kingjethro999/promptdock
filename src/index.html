        How PromptDock works

        Start with a rough idea. AI will draft a prompt, show its priorities, and flag details you may want to add. You can edit every part before using it.

        • Use the mic to record. Stop & send transcribes your speech with Groq, then turns the transcript into a prompt.
        • Sign in to save and sync your library across devices. Voice audio is sent to Groq only after Stop & send.
        • Publish a saved prompt to get a public link. Guests can view and copy it; signed-in users can save their own version.
        • Platform buttons copy your prompt and open the selected site.
