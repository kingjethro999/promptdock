Who runs PromptDock

PromptDock is an independent, open source personal project operated by King Jethro. This policy describes the hosted PromptDock website. People who run their own copy of the source code control that deployment’s data practices.

Information we handle

  • Account: email address, username, a password hash, verification and reset token hashes, sign-in sessions, device/browser user-agent text, and account timestamps.
  • Workspace: ideas and prompts you submit, saved prompts, tags, revisions, public prompt settings, and any feedback you send. Your current draft can also be stored in your browser.
  • AI and voice: text sent for generation or testing, plus audio you choose to send for transcription. Audio is sent when you select Stop & send; cancelling discards the recording in the browser.
  • Your providers: names, model choices, endpoint URLs, and API keys you save. Keys are encrypted on the server before storage.
  • Invitations and service protection: referral codes and which account invited a new account, verification status for referral rewards, and hashed identifiers used to enforce request limits. Hosting services may also keep technical request logs.

How we use it

We use this information to create and secure accounts, sync your library, deliver verification and password reset emails, generate or run prompts, transcribe recordings you send, display public prompts and invitations, calculate referral allowances, answer feedback, and protect the service from excessive requests. We do not sell account data or run advertising trackers in the PromptDock app.

Where it goes

The website and API run on Vercel, with PostgreSQL hosted on Render. Gmail is used to deliver account emails. Prompt and test text is sent to the configured AI provider, which can include Groq, Gemini, APMIX, or a provider you configure. Voice recordings are sent to Groq for transcription. Those providers may process submitted material under their own policies. Google Fonts may receive browser requests for fonts.

When you publish a prompt, anyone with its link can view and copy the finished prompt and may see your username. Your account email and private idea notes are not included in that public prompt view. Invitation pages may show your username, never your email. A recipient’s saved copy of a public prompt remains in their library even if you later unpublish yours.

Cookies and browser storage

PromptDock uses an HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in. Browser local storage keeps drafts and may temporarily keep older browser-only prompts until they are imported into an account. Browser session storage remembers an invitation code during signup. These are functional storage features; no advertising cookie is set by the app.

Retention and choices

Your account, saved prompts, and provider settings remain until you remove them or delete your account. The library keeps up to ten earlier prompt revisions. Session cookies expire after 30 days; verification links expire after 24 hours and reset links after one hour. Rate-limit records are periodically pruned. Feedback reports can remain after an account is deleted because their account link is removed, but a contact email entered in a report may remain until the report is removed.

You can edit your username, export or delete prompts, unpublish a public prompt, remove saved provider keys, end sessions, and delete your account in the app. Account deletion removes your account, sessions, prompts, revisions, and saved provider settings from the active database. Published copies already saved by other users, feedback reports, provider-side processing, and temporary hosting backups or logs may remain. To ask about access, correction, or removal of other information, use the contact options below.

Questions or requests

Use Report an issue or idea in the app, including a reply email if you want a response, or contact King Jethro on GitHub. GitHub comments are public, so avoid posting private account details there. PromptDock is not directed to children under 13; contact us if you believe a child has shared personal information. We may update this policy as the product changes and will change the effective date here.
